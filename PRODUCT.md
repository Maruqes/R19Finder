## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Manage vehicle profiles and car-part requests, research listings with configured AI providers, and retain the evidence and preferences behind each search.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Repository-confirmed workflows include request creation/editing with up to six photos; structured part facts and reviewed AI suggestions; seven-day session-owned drafts; car profiles and galleries; searches with Codex or Open WebUI; weekly schedules in Europe/Lisbon; listing blacklists and restoration; priority sources, language preferences and Discord notifications. Credentials stay on the server. Starting research can use provider credits.

The app is shared and has no user authentication. The Flask backend and PostgreSQL storage remain in place. Existing URLs, sessions and validation must be preserved.

## Brand Commitments

The user requested React and shadcn/ui, with Motion and Kokonut UI when useful. They explicitly chose: “Keep it dark throughout.” The product name remains R19 Finder and interface copy remains English.

## Evidence on Hand

Product and workflow documentation: README.md and api/docs/. Real vehicle and request data exists in the local database; do not fabricate records or substitute promotional imagery. Photos are user-provided and must retain their full proportions.
